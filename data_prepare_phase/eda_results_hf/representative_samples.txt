SYSTEMATICALLY SELECTED REPRESENTATIVE SAMPLES
================================================================================

SELECTION METHODOLOGY:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Random sampling: 100 reviews randomly selected from each category
2. Statistical filtering: Exclude outliers (< 10 words or > 300 words)
3. For mixed reviews: Prioritize samples with contrast markers
4. Stratified selection: Select samples across length spectrum:
   - Short: below median word count
   - Medium: median to 75th percentile
   - Long: above 75th percentile
5. Final output: 5 representative samples per category
